Privacy-First Technology

Complete Data Privacy

Your resume and job descriptions never leave your device. All AI processing happens locally with zero data collection or transmission.

Browser-Based AI

Advanced transformer models run directly in your browser using WebAssembly technology. No servers, no cloud processing, complete control.

Zero Data Retention

We don't store, log, or track any information. Your data is processed in memory and completely discarded when you close the browser tab.

Common Questions

Your data is completely secure because it never leaves your device. All AI processing happens locally in your browser using WebAssembly and ONNX Runtime. There are no servers, no data collection, and no tracking. You can even use the tool offline after the initial model download.
Our system uses advanced neural networks to analyze semantic meaning and context between your resume and job descriptions. Unlike simple keyword matching, our AI understands the relationships between skills, experience, and requirements to provide meaningful compatibility scores.
Most companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to screen resumes before they reach human recruiters. These systems scan for specific keywords, skills, and experience. Optimizing your resume helps ensure it passes these automated filters and gets seen by hiring managers.
Scores above 80% indicate strong alignment with the job requirements. However, use the score as guidance rather than a definitive measure. Focus on the specific recommendations provided to improve weak areas and highlight your most relevant experience.
This tool is completely free to use. It's an open-source project designed to help job seekers improve their applications. All processing happens in your browser at no cost, and we never collect or sell your personal data.
The first visit downloads a compact MiniLM model into your browser (about 25MB). Resume and job text never leave your device. After that, the model is cached and later analyses typically finish in under two seconds. The Run AI Analysis button stays disabled until the model is ready.
